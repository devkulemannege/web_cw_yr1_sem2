Database Systems

Details

The Database Systems course offers an intensive exploration into the architecture and management of organized data within the modern digital economy. In an era defined by "Big Data," the curriculum transitions from basic file storage to the The Database Systems course provides an intensive dive into the architecture and management of data within the modern digital economy. Transitioning from simple storage to sophisticated relational and NoSQL models, students learn to translate complex business needs into structured schemas using ER diagrams. By mastering normalization and SQL, learners ensure data integrity while performing high-speed queries. The curriculum emphasizes performance tuning through indexing and optimization, transforming sluggish systems into high-efficiency engines. Critical "ACID" properties are integrated to guarantee transactional reliability and data security. Students also explore document-based stores, gaining versatility across the entire data lifecycle. By the program's conclusion, participants evolve into "Data Architects" possessing the technical rigor to design, secure, and tune massive databases. They emerge equipped to support data-driven decision-making across any professional landscape in today's global enterprise environment.

Learning Objectives

  • Relational Modeling: Design complex schemas using ER Diagrams to map business logic.
  • Schema Normalization: Apply formal rules to eliminate redundancy and ensure structural integrity.
  • Advanced Querying: Master SQL for multi-table joins, filtering, and efficient data aggregation.
  • Transaction Management: Implement ACID properties for reliable processing and system recovery.
  • Performance Tuning: Optimize query speed and resource usage via indexing and plan analysis.
  • Security Protocols: Manage user permissions and encryption to protect against vulnerabilities.
  • NoSQL Exploration: Utilize document-based and distributed solutions for non-relational data.
  • Administrative Maintenance: Execute backup, recovery, and migration strategies for enterprise data.
